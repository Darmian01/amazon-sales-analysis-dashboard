# 📊 Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze Amazon sales performance, revenue, product ratings, discounts, customer reviews, best sellers, and profitability.



![Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard](![Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard](add.png))

---

##  Project Overview

This project analyzes Amazon product and sales data to uncover meaningful business insights and identify patterns in product performance, revenue generation, customer engagement, pricing, discounts, and profitability.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into an interactive visual reporting tool that can help businesses understand which products and categories are performing well and where opportunities for improvement exist.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Data visualization and dashboard development
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Measures and analytical calculations
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data preparation and analysis

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard provides insights into:

### 💰 Sales & Revenue
- Total sales
- Total revenue
- Revenue after discounts
- Revenue distribution
- Revenue contribution by product and seller

### ⭐ Product Performance
- Product ratings
- Most reviewed products
- Top-performing products
- Best-selling product categories

### 🏷️ Discount Analysis
- Discount levels
- Impact of discounts on purchases
- Comparison between discounted and non-discounted products

### 💎 Profitability
- Top profit-generating products
- Profit contribution by product category
- Identification of high-performing products

### 📊 Seller & Product Analysis
- Best sellers
- Organic vs sponsored products
- Product category performance
- Customer review activity
